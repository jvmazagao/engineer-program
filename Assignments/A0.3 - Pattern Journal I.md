# A0.3 — Pattern Journal I

## Why

Seniors don't re-derive every problem; they recognize which of a handful of patterns applies. 20 solved problems with written pattern notes builds the catalog — and the cold-solve session proves the notes are yours, not copy-paste.

## Brief

Create `dsa-from-scratch/02-pattern-journal/` and solve **20 problems** from the NeetCode roadmap — 8 from Arrays & Hashing, 6 from Two Pointers, 6 from Sliding Window. Defaults (swap any for a sibling problem from the same list):

- **Arrays & Hashing (8):** Two Sum, Valid Anagram, Contains Duplicate, Group Anagrams, Top K Frequent Elements, Product of Array Except Self, Longest Consecutive Sequence, Valid Sudoku
- **Two Pointers (6):** Valid Palindrome, Two Sum II, 3Sum, Container With Most Water, Remove Duplicates from Sorted Array, Trapping Rain Water (Hard — optional)
- **Sliding Window (6):** Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock, Longest Substring Without Repeating Characters, Longest Repeating Character Replacement, Permutation in String, Minimum Size Subarray Sum, Find All Anagrams in a String

Rules: try 25 minutes before opening any solution. One file per problem (`01-two-sum.py`…) with this **5-line note** at the top:

```
1. Pattern:
2. Key insight:
3. Time / space:
4. Mistake I made:
5. When I'd reach for it:
```

Keep a `NOTES.md` index: problem, pattern, solved-alone? (yes / with hint / needed solution — be honest).

**Peer part:** your peer swaps in 3 problems of their choosing from the same three categories and you solve them **cold** in a 45-min call while they watch — ~15 min each, no notes, no IDE autocomplete. The peer confirms your notes match how you actually solve, and writes a 3-line verdict in your submission note. **Mentor part:** mentor reviews the journal and the verdict in your 1:1.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All 20 problems attempted; unsolved ones flagged, not silently dropped
- [ ] Every note has all 5 lines, in your own words, with correct time/space
- [ ] Cold-solve: at least 2 of 3 problems solved within their 15-minute slots, thinking aloud
- [ ] Peer verdict confirms the notes match your actual solving
- [ ] Mentor sign-off in the 1:1

## Rubric (1–4)

| Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Coverage | Under 12 problems | 12–16 problems | 17–19 | All 20, honest status per problem |
| Note quality | Missing or copy-paste | Incomplete notes | All 5 lines, correct complexity | Notes show real insight and honest mistakes |
| Cold-solve | 0–1 of 3 solved | 2 of 3, struggled visibly | 2 of 3, clean approach | 3 of 3 with pattern named up front |
| Honesty | Notes claim solos that weren't | Vague statuses | Statuses accurate | Includes what was looked up and why |
