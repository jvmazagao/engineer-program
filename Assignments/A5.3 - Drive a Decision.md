# A5.3 — Drive a Decision

## Why

Stuck decisions don't unstick themselves — they rot into re-litigated PR threads and quiet resentment. The senior move isn't winning the argument; it's making the team *decide*, respectfully and on the record.

## Brief

Find a decision that's been stuck ≥2 weeks — a tech choice debated in Slack, an approach blocked on "we should discuss sometime", an argument that keeps resurfacing in review comments. Evidence it's stuck (links, dates of the last flare-up).

Drive it through five steps:

1. **Frame** — one paragraph: the decision to make, the options, and the default if we do nothing (plus the cost of delay)
2. **Map stakeholders** — who decides, who's affected, who can block
3. **Pre-wire** — 1:1 with ≥2 key people *before* any group discussion, including the strongest dissenter
4. **Converge** — run a short meeting or written thread with an explicit deadline: "we decide today / by Friday"
5. **Record** — ADR with the decision, owner, date, rejected options, and a revisit condition

Disagree-and-commit is a legitimate outcome. So is being overruled by the named decider. The decision does **not** have to go your way — a documented decision you drove is the deliverable.

## Tasks

- [ ] Name the stuck decision; evidence it's stuck with links and dates
- [ ] Write the one-paragraph frame: options, do-nothing default, cost of delay
- [ ] Map stakeholders: decider, affected, blockers
- [ ] Pre-wire 1:1s with ≥2 key people, including the strongest dissenter
- [ ] Convene the meeting or written thread with an explicit decision deadline
- [ ] Record the ADR: decision, owner, date, rejected options, revisit condition
- [ ] Add it to the Brag Document

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Decision documented and unblocked — work moving, or explicitly parked with an owner
- [ ] Pre-wiring happened before the group discussion
- [ ] The strongest dissenter agreed, disagreed-and-committed in writing, or was overruled by the named decider — not ignored
- [ ] ADR includes a revisit condition
- [ ] Outcome went your way or didn't — both acceptable; the record is the deliverable

## Rubric (1–4)

| Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Framing | Restated the argument | Options listed | Options + cost of delay + do-nothing default | Reframed from positions to a shared problem statement |
| Pre-wiring | None | Talked to allies only | Talked to the dissenters | Changed the proposal based on dissent *before* the meeting |
| Closure | Another meeting scheduled | Verbal decision | Written decision with an owner | Revisit condition named; dissent recorded respectfully |
| Sticks | Re-litigated within weeks | Held briefly | Held for a month+ | No re-litigation — the ADR settled it |
