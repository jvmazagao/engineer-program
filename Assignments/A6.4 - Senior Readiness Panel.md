# A6.4 — Senior Readiness Panel

## Why

The real promo panel is the worst possible first attempt. A mock panel with fresh-eyed seniors surfaces the gaps while they're still cheap to fix — and produces the written gap plan either way.

## Brief

**Session format — 45 min panel + 15 min private deliberation + written outcome.**

**Panel:** mentor + 1–2 senior/staff engineers who haven't been closely involved with your work (fresh eyes matter). They receive the A6.3 packet link 48h ahead.

| Minutes | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0–10 | Engineer presents the phase-6 project (or strongest body of work): problem, approach, measured impact |
| 10–30 | **Design deep-dive** — panel drills into the presented work and adjacent decisions: "why this and not X", "what breaks at 10×", "what would you change now" |
| 30–40 | **Behavioral** — influence without authority, disagreement, failure stories; panel probes for senior vs. mid signals: ownership of outcomes, lifting others, handling ambiguity |
| 40–45 | Engineer asks questions; panel closes |

Then 15 minutes deliberation *without* the engineer. Each panelist scores against the Competency Matrix pillars, 1–4 each.

**Written outcome:** verdict — **ready / ready with gaps / not yet** — plus a gap plan: each gap concrete, with a closing action and timeline.

## Tasks

- [ ] Recruit the panel: mentor + 1–2 seniors not closely involved with your work
- [ ] Prepare the 10-min presentation from the A6.3 packet; rehearse once, timed
- [ ] Prepare the 3 hardest "why not X" questions per project claim
- [ ] Send the packet link to panelists ≥48h ahead; run the panel
- [ ] Receive the written verdict + gap plan
- [ ] Convert the gap plan into next-cycle goals; re-score the Competency Matrix
- [ ] Add the outcome to the Brag Document

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Panel ran 45 min with ≥1 panelist who isn't the mentor
- [ ] Presentation finished in ≤10 minutes
- [ ] Written verdict recorded: ready / ready with gaps / not yet
- [ ] Gap plan written: each gap concrete, with a closing action and timeline
- [ ] Panelist scores against Matrix pillars recorded
- [ ] Outcome translated into next-cycle goals regardless of verdict

## Rubric (1–4)

| Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Presentation | Over time, dense | On time, readable | On time, impact-led | Panelists restated the impact correctly in deliberation |
| Deep-dive | Defended by repetition | Answered surface drills | Answered with trade-offs | Changed own position live when the panel's argument was better |
| Behavioral | Anecdotes without role clarity | Stories with outcomes | Shows ownership of outcomes and others' growth | Senior-level signals appeared unprompted |
| Outcome use | Verdict only | Gaps listed | Gap plan with actions | Plan became next cycle's goals with dates |

## Mentor review prompts

- In deliberation: "Where did you hear 'I' when it should have been 'we' — or the reverse?"
- "Which answer would you not have gotten from a mid engineer?"
- If not yet: "What is the single gap that most blocks it?"
- If ready: "What would a *staff*-level gap look like?"

## Stretch 🔸

- If the verdict is ready, start the staff-path plan early: pick the next ladder rung's first gap and take one step toward it this quarter.
