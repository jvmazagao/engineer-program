# Phase 6 — Scope and Promotion (Months 14–15)

← [[05 - Phase 5 - Influence and Communication]] · Back to [[Mid to Senior Engineer Roadmap]]

> This phase **starts on day 1**. The brag doc and the hunt for a senior-scope project run through the whole plan. Months 14–15 are when you deliver and package the evidence.

**Goal:** show sustained senior-level impact on work that matters to the business, and make it legible to the people who decide promotions.

**Deliverable:** a senior-scope project shipped end to end + a promotion packet built from your [[Brag Document]].

## Day 1 (do now, not in month 11)

- [ ] Create [[Brag Document]]; update every Friday
- [ ] Read your company's career ladder; map each senior expectation to the [[Senior Engineer Competency Matrix]]
- [ ] Tell your manager explicitly: "I want to reach senior in ~15 months. What gaps do you see?"
- [ ] Agree on a monthly checkpoint

## Finding a senior-scope project (months 4–9)

A good candidate project has:
- **Ambiguity:** the problem is clear, the solution isn't
- **Business impact:** revenue, cost, reliability, customer pain, or developer velocity, and you can measure it
- **Breadth:** touches more than one component or team
- **Duration:** roughly 1–3 months
- **Visibility:** leadership cares about the outcome

Where to look: recurring incidents, top customer complaints, slow/painful dev workflows, cost spikes, tech debt blocking the roadmap.

## Month 14 — Execute

### Week 53 — Frame the project
- [ ] One-pager: problem, impact metric, scope, non-goals, milestones, risks
- [ ] Get manager + key stakeholder sign-off

### Week 54 — Plan and delegate
- [ ] Break into milestones that each deliver value; identify the riskiest part and do it first
- [ ] Involve others (mentee, peers); you own the outcome, not every line of code

### Week 55 — Drive execution
- [ ] Weekly written updates; surface risks early; adjust scope, not quality
- [ ] Unblock others before writing your own code

### Week 56 — Land it
- [ ] Rollout plan, monitoring, rollback; announce; measure impact against the baseline

## Month 15 — Make it visible

### Week 57 — Retrospective
- [ ] Project retro with the team: what went well, what to change
- [ ] Write the impact summary: before/after numbers

### Week 58 — Promotion packet
- [ ] For each ladder expectation: 1–2 concrete examples from the brag doc with evidence (links to RFCs, PRs, dashboards, postmortems)
- [ ] Write in terms of **impact**, not activity ("reduced p99 by 40%, unblocking X" not "worked on performance")

### Week 59 — Feedback loop
- [ ] Ask 3–5 peers for written feedback (manager can request it)
- [ ] Review the packet with your manager; identify remaining gaps honestly

### Week 60 — Next 12 months
- [ ] Re-score the [[Senior Engineer Competency Matrix]]
- [ ] Whether or not the promo lands this cycle, set the next plan (close gaps or start a staff-path plan)

## Self-check

- [ ] I led a project from ambiguous problem to measured impact
- [ ] My manager can explain my impact without me in the room
- [ ] Every claim in my packet links to evidence
- [ ] I know exactly which gaps remain, if any
