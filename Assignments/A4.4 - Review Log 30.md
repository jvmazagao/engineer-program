## Why

Most engineers believe they review for correctness and mostly review for style. Thirty logged reviews replace that belief with data — and the one comment you keep writing is a lint rule waiting to happen.

## Brief

Over weeks 41–44, log **every substantive review** as you do it — same day, not reconstructed on Friday:

| PR link | Issues found | Category per issue | Minutes spent |

Categories: correctness · concurrency · design · tests · naming · style · performance · none.

Target: **30 logged reviews**. Reviews where you found nothing count too — they're the interesting ones.

**Self-analysis** at the end:

- What's your category distribution? (Most engineers discover they're style-heavy.)
- Which categories *never* appear — do you not see them, or do your team's PRs not contain them?
- Compare against your A4.3 misses: did the seeded-bug gaps show up in real reviews?

Then ask one peer to look at your distribution and name what they'd add.

**Automate one recurring comment:** if you've written the same comment ≥3 times, turn it into a linter rule, CI check, or PR template item — and verify it catches the case that prompted it.

## Tasks

- [ ] Create the log table; record each review the day it happens
- [ ] 30 reviews logged: PR link, issues with categories, minutes spent
- [ ] Self-analysis: category distribution, blind spots, comparison with A4.3 misses
- [ ] One peer reviews your distribution and adds what you can't see
- [ ] Pick the most-repeated comment (≥3×) and automate it (lint rule / CI check / PR template)
- [ ] Verify the automation catches the original case

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] 30 reviews logged with dates spread over the period — not batch-entered at the end
- [ ] Every issue categorized; "no issues" reviews recorded as such
- [ ] Self-analysis names the weakest category with a hypothesis why
- [ ] Peer sanity-check on the analysis recorded
- [ ] One automation live in CI/lint config, with a link
- [ ] The automation demonstrably catches at least the original case

## Rubric (1–4)

| Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Logging | <30 or reconstructed | 30 but sparse | Complete with categories | Also tracks time spent and depth per review |
| Self-analysis | Totals only | Distribution noted | Blind spots named with hypotheses | Hypotheses tested — e.g. reread a PR you rated clean and find what you missed |
| Automation | Idea only | Rule written | Rule live in CI, catching the case | Rule + short doc so the team knows why it exists |
| Transfer | Personal notes | Findings shared | Team checklist updated | Findings changed how at least one peer reviews |
