# A6.3 — Impact Summary and Promotion Packet

## Why

Promotion cases are won on evidence, not adjectives. The packet turns fifteen months of [[Brag Document]] entries into a stranger-verifiable case that your manager can defend without you in the room.

## Brief

Two artifacts, built from the Brag Document (bring it current first — every Friday accounted for).

**1. Impact summary** — one page:

- The 2–3 projects with before/after numbers
- Design decisions, the production record, people impact (mentoring, reviews, decisions driven)
- Impact language throughout: *"reduced p99 by 40%, unblocking X"* — never *"worked on performance"*

**2. The packet** — for each pillar of the [[Senior Engineer Competency Matrix]] (2–6, plus 0–1 where relevant):

- 1–2 concrete examples from the brag doc
- **Every claim linked to evidence**: RFC, PR, dashboard, postmortem, sent update, mentee artifact
- A claim with no link gets evidence found — or gets cut
- Self-score the Matrix with evidence links in the column; leave the honest lows visible

**The challenge round:** one peer + the mentor read it and mark every claim they find unsupported or inflated. Each marked claim is resolved with evidence or cut. No orphan claims survive.

Finish with a manager walkthrough against the actual ladder wording — not your memory of it.

## Tasks

- [ ] Bring the Brag Document current
- [ ] Write the impact summary: one page, numbers per project, impact verbs
- [ ] Map each pillar to 1–2 examples with evidence links
- [ ] Self-score the Competency Matrix with links in the evidence column
- [ ] Peer + mentor challenge round: mark unsupported/inflated claims
- [ ] Resolve every mark: evidence found or claim cut
- [ ] Walk through with your manager against the real ladder

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every claim links to evidence — zero exceptions
- [ ] Impact phrasing throughout (outcome + number), no activity lists
- [ ] All six pillars covered with 1–2 examples each
- [ ] Self-scored Matrix included, with honest lows visible
- [ ] Every challenged claim resolved with evidence or cut
- [ ] Manager walkthrough done against the actual ladder wording

## Rubric (1–4)

| Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence | Claims without links | Some links | Every claim linked | Links verifiable by a stranger without you explaining them |
| Impact language | Activity lists | Some outcomes | Outcomes with numbers | Numbers carry baselines and attribution ("from X to Y, I did Z") |
| Coverage | One strength, repeated | 4 pillars | All 6 pillars | Includes a real weakness with a closing plan |
| Honesty | Inflated | Slightly rosy | Accurate | The challenge round found nothing to cut — and you expected some |
