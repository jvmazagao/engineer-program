# A5.4 — Mentoring Log

## Why

The step from mid to senior happens when your output stops being measured in PRs. Mentoring is the cleanest test: can someone else do things they couldn't do before, and can you show it was because of you?

## Brief

Start biweekly 30-minute 1:1s with a junior or mid engineer. They must volunteer — ask, don't assign. Minimum commitment: 8 weeks, ≥4 sessions.

**Session 1:** agree 1–2 growth goals the *mentee* owns — from their ambitions, not your agenda. "Learn X" handed down by you doesn't count.

**The log** — one entry per session: date, what we discussed, goal progress, feedback given/received, blockers removed.

**Feedback:** give at least one piece of SBI feedback (Situation → Behavior → Impact), within 48h of the triggering event. Positive and constructive both count; at least one constructive one is expected.

**Growth evidence** at the end: before/after on the agreed goals, in the mentee's own words where possible — ask them for a quote. Independent signals beat your opinion: shipped work, others noticing, them handling something solo.

Also record what the mentee says *you* should do differently as a mentor — and what you did about it.

## Tasks

- [ ] Ask a junior/mid to mentor; agree biweekly 30-min 1:1s for ≥8 weeks
- [ ] Session 1: agree 1–2 growth goals; write them down together
- [ ] Keep the log per session: topics, goal progress, actions
- [ ] Give ≥1 SBI feedback within 48h of the situation; log it
- [ ] Remove at least one real blocker for them (intro, unblocking a PR, providing context)
- [ ] Write the growth evidence: before/after on goals, mentee's own words included
- [ ] Ask what you should do differently as a mentor; record and act on it

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] ≥4 sessions over ≥8 weeks, logged with dates
- [ ] Goals written and owned by the mentee
- [ ] ≥1 SBI feedback logged with situation, behavior, and impact stated separately
- [ ] One concrete blocker removed, with link or evidence
- [ ] Growth evidence includes the mentee's own words
- [ ] Feedback-about-you-as-mentor recorded and acted on or explained

## Rubric (1–4)

| Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cadence | Sessions drifted | Held the cadence | Held cadence with an agenda per session | Mentee drives the agenda by the end |
| Feedback | Praise only, vague | Constructive given, not SBI | Proper SBI within 48h | Mentee now asks for feedback proactively — the channel is open |
| Growth evidence | "They're doing great" | One anecdote | Before/after on the agreed goals | Independent evidence: shipped work, others noticing |
| Mentee ownership | You assigned the goals | Shared goals | Mentee-owned goals | Mentee solves problems you'd have solved for them |

## Mentor review prompts

- "What did the mentee do this month that they couldn't have done two months ago?"
- "Read your SBI note aloud: is the Behavior sentence about observable actions only?"
- "Where did you give the answer instead of the question that leads to it?"
- "What did they teach you?"
