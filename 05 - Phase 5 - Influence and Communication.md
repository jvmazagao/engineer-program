## Month 12 — Writing and speaking

### Week 45 — Clear writing
- [ ] BLUF (bottom line up front), audience-first, one idea per paragraph
- 🧪 Rewrite one of your past RFCs/Slack threads in BLUF style

### Week 46 — Communicating up and out
- [ ] Translate tech to impact: risk, cost, time, customer outcome
- [ ] Status updates: progress, risks, asks. Bad news early.
- 🧪 Write a weekly project update for a non-engineering stakeholder

### Week 47 — Presenting
- [ ] Patrick Winston talk; structure: problem → why it matters → what we did → what's next
- 🧪 Give a 20-min brown-bag (e.g. your Phase 1 deep-dive or Phase 3 postmortem)

### Week 48 — Disagreement and decisions
- [ ] Disagree and commit; separate people from positions; decision records
- [ ] Facilitate a meeting that ends with a decision and owners
- 🧪 Drive one stuck technical decision to closure

## Month 13 — Multiplying others

### Week 49 — Mentoring
- [ ] Mentoring vs coaching vs sponsoring
- [ ] Ask questions before giving answers; let them own the solution
- 🧪 Start biweekly 1:1s with a junior/mid engineer; agree on 1–2 growth goals

### Week 50 — Feedback
- [ ] SBI model (Situation, Behavior, Impact); give feedback within 48 h
- 🧪 Give one piece of positive and one piece of constructive feedback to a peer
- 🧪 Ask your manager and 2 peers: "What's one thing I should do differently?"

### Week 51 — Cross-team work
- [ ] Map stakeholders: who's affected, who decides, who can block
- [ ] Pre-wire: talk 1:1 before the big meeting
- 🧪 Lead a change that needs another team's buy-in

### Week 52 — Onboarding and docs
- 🧪 Improve onboarding for your service (README, architecture diagram, runbooks)
- 🧪 Pair with your mentee on a task they own; observe, don't drive

## Self-check

- [ ] Non-engineers understand my updates without follow-up questions
- [ ] I can drive a meeting to a decision
- [ ] Someone I mentored can show concrete growth
- [ ] I've given hard feedback respectfully and received it without getting defensive
