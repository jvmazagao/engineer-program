# A6.2 — Weekly Project Updates

← [[06 - Phase 6 - Scope and Promotion]] · Catalog: [[Assignments.base]]

## Why

Projects go sideways in silence: the engineer knows, nobody else does, and the stakeholder learns about the slip the week it lands. A fixed weekly cadence with risks on top is the cheapest insurance a project lead can buy.

## Brief

For **4 consecutive weeks**, send a written update on the phase-6 project to the stakeholder list (manager, affected team, anyone who asked). Same day each week, same format, under 200 words — link, don't attach:

- **Progress** — what shipped or changed, against the one-pager's milestones
- **Risks** — what could slip it, with likelihood and the plan
- **Asks** — what you need, from whom, by when

Rules:

- Bad news first
- A week with no progress says so, and why
- A risk never appears in the same update that announces it landed — it's tracked from the update before
- Every ask names a person and a date

Track whether asks got answered; report the outcomes in later updates.

## Tasks

- [ ] Agree the send day and distribution list; first update goes out on schedule
- [ ] Send 4 weekly updates, each: progress vs. milestones, risks with plans, asks with owners and dates
- [ ] Keep risk tracking continuous — nothing vanishes between updates
- [ ] Ensure at least one update contains bad news (or explicitly note that none occurred)
- [ ] Track responses to each ask; report outcomes in later updates

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] 4 updates sent on the same weekday each week
- [ ] Each under 200 words, three sections in order
- [ ] No risk disappears between updates without resolution or escalation
- [ ] Every ask names a person and a date
- [ ] At least one update led with bad news or an explicit "no progress because X"
- [ ] Recipient response (or silence) noted per ask

## Rubric (1–4)

| Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cadence | Missed weeks | Sent but irregular | 4 on schedule | A reader could set a calendar reminder on you |
| Risk honesty | Risks appear when they hit | Risks listed, vague | Risks with likelihood + plan | A risk surfaced early enough that the ask prevented the hit |
| Asks | "Let me know if…" | Asks without owner/date | Named person + date | Ask was answered — and you reported the outcome |
| Signal density | Activity list | Milestone references | Variance vs. plan visible | A busy stakeholder gets the project state in 30 seconds |

## Submit

## Mentor review prompts

- "Which sentence is there to make you look good? Cut it."
- "Did any ask get ignored? What did you do — escalate or drop it?"
- "Show me the risk that was hardest to write. Was it early enough?"

## Stretch 🔸

- Ask a recipient to estimate project health from update #4 alone, before reading the rest — check their read against reality.
