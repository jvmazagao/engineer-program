# A3.5 — Blameless Postmortem

← [[03 - Phase 3 - Production Ownership]] · Catalog: [[Assignments.base]]

| Phase | Due | Format | Effort | Reviewed by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 — Production Ownership | end of week 34 | ✍️ written | ~4 h | peer+mentor |

## Why

A postmortem that names a person ends investigation at the person. A blameless one asks what made the action reasonable at the time — and that's where the fixes actually are.

## Brief

Pick a real incident you were involved in — or the A3.4 game day. Use your team's postmortem template if one exists; otherwise this structure:

- **Summary** — 2–3 sentences a VP could read
- **Impact** — users affected, duration, numbers (failed requests, tickets). If you can't quantify, say why not
- **Timeline** — detection → mitigation → resolution, timestamped, reconstructed from logs/alerts/chat, not memory; cite the source per entry
- **Contributing factors** — plural, always. No single root cause. Span ≥2 categories (technical, process, communication). If a person's action contributed, ask what made that action reasonable at the time
- **What went well** — the detection/mitigation that worked
- **Action items** — each with an owner and a due date, typed as prevent / detect / mitigate. At least one must make the incident *less likely*, not just faster to notice

Blameless means systems and conditions, not people. Have the mentor read specifically for blame language before it goes to the team.

## Tasks

- [ ] Choose the incident; agree the postmortem with the team (don't write it in secret)
- [ ] Reconstruct the timeline from logs, alerts, and chat — cite a source per entry
- [ ] Quantify impact, or explain why quantifying is impossible
- [ ] List ≥3 contributing factors across ≥2 categories
- [ ] Write action items: owner + due date + prevent/detect/mitigate for each
- [ ] Mentor blame-language pass; fix, then share with the team
- [ ] Track the action items; mark completed ones as they land

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Timeline has timestamps and cites sources (logs/chats/dashboards)
- [ ] Impact quantified, or the impossibility of quantifying explained
- [ ] ≥3 contributing factors, ≥2 categories, no person blamed
- [ ] Every action item has an owner and due date; ≥1 prevents recurrence rather than detecting it
- [ ] Mentor read-through removed all blame language before team review
- [ ] Action items are tracked — not filed and forgotten

## Rubric (1–4)

| Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Timeline | From memory, vague | Rough order of events | Timestamped with sources | Includes the misleading signals that sent responders the wrong way |
| Analysis | Single root cause: "the bug" | Two factors, both technical | ≥3 factors across ≥2 categories | Explains why each factor was reasonable at the time |
| Blamelessness | Names people | "Someone should have…" | Systems-focused throughout | Examines own actions without self-flagellation |
| Action items | "Add monitoring" | Owners missing | Owner + date on each | Prevent/detect/mitigate mix, and at least one already shipped |
